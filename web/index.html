How OneMind works

  1. Submit ideas anonymously. Every participant proposes privately — no anchoring, no groupthink, no social pressure. Ideas stand on their own merit, not on who said them.
  2. Rate every other idea on a structured grid. Compare proposals against each other instead of debating them. The grid forces clear preferences and surfaces the strongest ideas without facilitator bias.
  3. Watch convergence happen. When the same idea wins multiple rounds in a row, that's genuine group consensus — a decision your team can stand behind, with a transparent record of exactly how you got there.

  • Fully asynchronous — participants contribute on their own schedule, no calendar coordination

  • Transparent record of how the decision was reached, defensible after the fact
